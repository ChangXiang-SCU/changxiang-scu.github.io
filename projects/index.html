CHI 2024

Gesture-Based Interaction between AVs and Pedestrians

A virtual-hand eHMI that lets an autonomous vehicle gesture its yielding intent to pedestrians, tested in VR (N = 31) and an online survey (N = 394).
HFES ASPIRE 2025

Pedestrian Trust after Interacting with Robotaxis on Public Roads

An on-road study of how pedestrians' trust in AVs forms through repeated crossings in front of Level-4 robotaxis at an uncontrolled intersection.
Under review

Social Situation Awareness in Driver-Pedestrian Interactions

A dyadic VR study of 31 driver-pedestrian pairs that measures situation awareness as a property of the pair, not only of each road user.
